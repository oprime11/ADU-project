ADU Compass — three designs, one mission

A homeowner's complete guide to designing and building an accessory dwelling unit in San Mateo County: costs, timeline, sizes, permits, taxes, rental income, an address-based lot visualizer, and recommended local builders. The same content and live calculators, presented three very different ways.

💡 Open each design and try it: enter an address in the map section, drag the ADU footprint around your yard, and move the cost sliders. All three share one data engine (assets/adu-data.js) so estimates always agree.
